10+Years of
Craft
SINCE 2016

Crafted with Intention

FA Fashion began as a single tailoring counter in Lahore, founded by Fahad Ali on a simple belief: clothing should make people feel more like themselves, not less.

A decade later, that same philosophy shapes every stitch — from our Men's formalwear to Kids' everyday essentials. We work with fewer, better suppliers, and we design for how people actually live.

Today, FA Fashion serves customers across Pakistan and beyond, but the counter mindset never left: every order still gets checked by hand before it ships.

MILESTONES

Our Journey

2016

The First Counter

Fahad Ali opens a single tailoring stall in Lahore's Anarkali Bazaar, focused on made-to-fit formalwear.

2019

First Flagship Store

FA Fashion opens its first full retail location, expanding into Women's and Kids' lines.

2022

Online Launch

The FA Fashion online store goes live, bringing the in-store experience to customers nationwide.

2026

150+ Brand Partners

FA Fashion now curates over 1,000 products from 150+ trusted brands, serving 50,000+ customers.
